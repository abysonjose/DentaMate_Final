 Audit Guidelines

Audit Log Purpose:

  • Track all financial transactions and modifications
  • Maintain compliance with regulatory requirements
  • Support internal and external audit processes
  • Provide accountability and transparency

Key Information Tracked:

  • User Actions: Who performed the action
  • Timestamps: When the action occurred
  • Entity Changes: What was modified
  • Details: Specific changes made

Accountant Responsibilities:

  • Review audit logs for unusual activities
  • Add clarifying notes for complex transactions
  • Export logs for external audit requirements
  • Flag discrepancies for management review
