 Cashier Coordination Guidelines

Payment Reconciliation Process:

  • Review all cashier payments for accuracy and completeness
  • Match payment receipts with corresponding billing records
  • Verify payment amounts and modes are correctly recorded
  • Flag any discrepancies for immediate resolution

Cash Handover Verification:

  • Verify cash amounts match transaction records
  • Check for proper documentation and receipts
  • Ensure all transactions are accounted for
  • Report any discrepancies to management

Communication with Cashiers:

  • Provide timely feedback on reconciliation issues
  • Request clarification for unclear transactions
  • Maintain professional communication standards
  • Document all interactions for audit purposes
