 Billing Staff Coordination Guidelines

Bill Validation Process:

  • Review all service codes and quantities for accuracy
  • Verify pricing against approved service rates
  • Check discount applications and approvals
  • Validate tax calculations and compliance
  • Ensure proper documentation is attached

Quality Control Checks:

  • Cross-reference with doctor's treatment notes
  • Verify patient insurance coverage and claims
  • Check for duplicate billing entries
  • Validate department and cost center assignments

Communication with Billing Staff:

  • Provide clear feedback on validation results
  • Request specific corrections when needed
  • Document all interactions for audit purposes
  • Escalate persistent issues to management
